Sound effects (assets/audio/), all CC0 1.0 public domain unless noted. Converted to mono MP3 or WAV with ffmpeg (trimmed, no other changes):

- Kenney, Sci-Fi Sounds (https://opengameart.org/content/sci-fi-sounds): crash_big.mp3 (impactMetal_000), smash.mp3 (explosionCrunch_000), monster_hit.mp3 (slime_000), monster_smash.mp3 (lowFrequency_explosion_000), turbo.mp3 (thrusterFire_000), boost.mp3 (thrusterFire_001).
- Kenney, 50 RPG Sound Effects (https://opengameart.org/content/50-rpg-sound-effects): coin.mp3 (handleCoins2), coins_big.mp3 (handleCoins), door.mp3 (metalLatch).
- Kenney, Digital Sound Effects (https://opengameart.org/content/63-digital-sound-effects-lasers-phasers-space-etc): upgrade.mp3 (powerUp2), ring.mp3 (powerUp7), zap.mp3 (zapThreeToneUp), hop.mp3 (phaserUp1).
- Kenney, Interface Sounds (https://opengameart.org/content/interface-sounds; copy used: https://github.com/Calinou/kenney-interface-sounds): activity_start.mp3 (confirmation_001), comms.mp3 (maximize_001), click.mp3 (click_001).
- Kenney, Short Music Jingles (https://opengameart.org/content/85-short-music-jingles): pickup.mp3 (jingles_NES09), activity_done.mp3 (jingles_NES03), event_win.mp3 (jingles_PIZZA02).
- Made for this game from code by tools/make_sounds.js (CC0): klaxon.mp3, boing.mp3.
